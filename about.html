About xDel | SO2

xDel is a Standoff 2 player and content creator. The channel started as a way to save the best rounds from everyday matches — now it is a daily stream of short highlights and a growing community of players.

The channel

Every video comes from real matches played by xDel. Each short shows one round from start to finish: the entry, the kills and the result, without cuts inside the action. Videos are published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram several times a day.

The community

The xDel | SO2 Discord server brings viewers together: team up for matches, post your own clips, take part in weekly games with xDel and get help with your account progress.

Plans

  • Longer videos: case openings, skin collections and full matches with commentary.
  • Live streams with the community.
  • Viewer clip of the week — the best moment sent by a subscriber gets featured on the channel.
