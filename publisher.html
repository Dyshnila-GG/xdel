xDel Publisher

The in-house tool the xDel | SO2 creator uses to prepare, schedule and publish his own short gaming videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram from one place.

Features

One upload, every platform

Pick a finished short once and publish it to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels together.

Scheduling

Choose "publish now", an exact date and time, or the next free daily time slot.

Captions per platform

Titles, descriptions and hashtags are prepared separately for each platform and can be edited before publishing.

First comment

A first comment with a question for viewers is added under each new video.

Discord announcement

When a new YouTube video goes live, an announcement is posted on the creator's own Discord server.

History

Every publication is logged with its status on each platform, so nothing is posted twice.

How it works with TikTok

  1. Connect. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes his own account. The app reads only basic profile info (name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  2. Prepare. He selects a finished short, edits the caption and hashtags and chooses the privacy setting.
  3. Publish. The video is sent to his TikTok profile with the Content Posting API — either posted directly or uploaded to TikTok drafts for final editing in the TikTok app.
  4. Track. The app shows the post status and the creator's own public videos and basic stats so he can see how each short performs.

xDel Publisher is used only by the owner of the xDel | SO2 channels for his own accounts. It is not offered to other users and never posts to anyone else's account.

Data and privacy

Access tokens and settings are stored on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
