Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how xDel Publisher ("the app"), a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the xDel | SO2 channels, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner to manage his own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Discord). It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the owner's connected accounts (name, avatar, account ID).
  • Permissions to upload videos, publish posts and add comments on the owner's own accounts.
  • Basic statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments), if available.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos, captions and comments at the times he chooses, and to show him the status of those posts.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and settings are stored locally on the owner's computer. The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Video descriptions may be sent to OpenAI to help draft captions; no account credentials are sent.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions, in TikTok and Instagram app settings, or by disconnecting the account in the app.
